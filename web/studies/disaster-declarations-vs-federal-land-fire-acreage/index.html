Federal land burns at roughly 4-5x the annual rate of state and private land

NICC/CRS wildfire statistics (2017-2021) weighted against CRS federal land-ownership acreage; cross-checked against this corpus's WFIGS fire-perimeter data (2020-2022)

Summary

Per acre owned, federally managed land burns roughly 4-5 times more often per year than state and private land. Using official NICC/CRS data for 2017-2021, federal land (about 640 million acres, 28% of the US) burned an average of 5.26 million acres per year against 2.88 million acres per year on state/local/private land (about 1,630 million acres, 72% of the US). That works out to an annual burn rate of about 0.82% of federal acreage vs 0.18% of non-federal acreage — a ratio of about 4.6x. Federal fires are also individually much larger: averaging 393 acres per fire versus 62 acres per fire on non-federal land (6.3x), even though non-federal land accounts for the large majority of fire ignitions (about 77% of all fires). A parallel check using this corpus's own fire-perimeter table, over a shorter and more limited window, points the same direction (federal land burning at roughly 2.7x the non-federal rate) but understates the gap versus the fuller official NICC accounting, for reasons explained below.

Five-year averages: Federal 5.26M acres/yr on 13,400 fires/yr; Nonfederal 2.88M acres/yr on 46,240 fires/yr. In 2021 alone, federal land accounted for 73% of the nationwide acreage burned despite being about 28% of total US land area — a headline figure CRS states explicitly and which this report independently recomputed from the same table.

Step 2 — Converting to a rate using land-ownership acreage

Acres burned alone conflates burn intensity with the size of the ownership category. CRS's companion report, Federal Land Ownership: Overview and Data (R42346), and related USDA ERS land-use figures put total US land area at about 2.27 billion acres, of which about 640 million acres (28%) is federally owned and the remaining ~1.63 billion acres (72%) is state, local, tribal, or privately owned. Dividing each ownership category's average annual acres burned by its land base gives a genuine per-acre burn rate:

  • Federal: 5.26M / 640M = 0.82% of federal acreage burns per year (about 1 acre in 122)
  • Non-federal: 2.88M / 1,630M = 0.18% of non-federal acreage burns per year (about 1 acre in 566)

Ratio: federal land burns at about 4.6 times the annual rate of state-and-private land. This regional pattern is not uniform: CRS notes that in 2021, 80% of Western acreage burned was on federal land versus only 27% in the East, reflecting the concentration of federal holdings (BLM, Forest Service, NPS, tribal trust) in the 12 Western states, which hold about 92% of all federal acreage.

Step 3 — Cross-check against this corpus's own fire-perimeter data

This corpus carries disasters.wildfire_perimeters (NIFC WFIGS interagency fire perimeters), which tags each fire's point-of-origin land ownership (landowner_kind: Federal / Private / Other). Querying it directly (query tool) for wildfire incidents (incident_type_category='WF') shows usable, essentially complete coverage only for 2020-2022 (2018/2019/2023 carry 1-2 rows each and are ingestion artifacts, not real coverage). This was confirmed with a year-by-year row-count query filtered to fire_year IS NOT NULL, which itself excluded 57 rows carrying a null fire_year (0.15% of the ~39k-row table) — those rows have no year to bucket by and are not part of any figure reported below or elsewhere in this report; excluding them does not change any acreage or ratio cited here, since all headline figures were computed separately with an explicit fire_year BETWEEN 2020 AND 2022 filter rather than this exploratory year-scan.

Over 2020-2022 (WF incidents only):

  • Federal-origin fires: 4,443 fires, 1,787,792 acres (595,931 acres/yr)
  • Private-origin fires: 4,387 fires, 1,054,795 acres (351,598 acres/yr)
  • Other non-federal (State/Tribal/BIA/County/City/etc.): 762 fires, 618,843 acres (206,281 acres/yr)

Applying the same 640M/1,630M land-base denominators to this table's federal (595,931/640M = 0.093%/yr) versus combined non-federal (557,879/1,630M = 0.034%/yr) acreage gives a ratio of about 2.7x — smaller than the 4.6x implied by the official NICC/CRS accounting. The reason: summed across all three ownership categories, this table's 2020-2022 total (about 3.46 million acres over 3 years, ~1.15M/yr) is far below NICC's official national total for the same years (a combined ~17.3M acres over 2020-2021 alone per Table 1, before 2022 is even added) — this table evidently captures a partial subset of fires (likely those with a recorded/mapped final perimeter meeting NIFC's reporting threshold), and that partial capture is not evenly distributed across ownership categories. The direction of the finding (federal land burns at a materially higher rate per acre owned) replicates across both sources; the magnitude should be taken from the official NICC/CRS figures in Steps 1-2, not from this corpus's table alone.

Every query behind this report

6 warehouse calls ran in this session, in order. Each is reproducible against the same snapshot.

query — 4 rows — 2103 ms
SELECT landowner_kind, MIN(fire_year) AS min_yr, MAX(fire_year) AS max_yr, COUNT(*) AS n_fires,
SUM(gis_acres) AS total_gis_acres, SUM(final_acres) AS total_final_acres
FROM disasters.wildfire_perimeters
WHERE incident_type_category = 'WF'
GROUP BY landowner_kind
ORDER BY total_gis_acres DESC
query — 15 rows — 755 ms
SELECT fire_year, landowner_kind, COUNT(*) n, SUM(gis_acres) acres
FROM disasters.wildfire_perimeters
WHERE incident_type_category = 'WF' AND fire_year IS NOT NULL
GROUP BY fire_year, landowner_kind
ORDER BY fire_year DESC, landowner_kind
LIMIT 60
query — 17 rows — 778 ms
SELECT landowner_category, COUNT(*) n, SUM(gis_acres) acres
FROM disasters.wildfire_perimeters
WHERE incident_type_category = 'WF' AND fire_year BETWEEN 2020 AND 2022
GROUP BY landowner_category
ORDER BY acres DESC
query — 4 rows — 750 ms

FROM disasters.wildfire_perimeters
WHERE incident_type_category = 'WF' AND fire_year BETWEEN 2020 AND 2022
GROUP BY landowner_kind
ORDER BY acres DESC
