Small-business approval rates by tract minority status cannot be computed from public data; a mortgage proxy shows denial rates about 6.5 points higher in majority-minority tracts

Answer

It cannot be measured directly today. An approval rate needs applications and denials by tract. Public small-business data (CRA, SBA 7(a)/504 approvals in fiscal.sba_loan_approvals) records only loans made or approved. CFPB Section 1071 would supply tract-level action taken, but compliance dates have been pushed back and no approval-rate analysis by tract type has been published. Published evidence is about loan shares: Woodstock Institute found predominantly minority tracts in Chicago had about 15% of businesses but about 8% of small loans under $100,000 (a share gap, not an approval rate).

Proxy (mortgages, not small business): in HMDA 2023 loan-level data, across 525 counties with at least 200 decided applications in both tract groups, the denial rate (denials / (originations + denials)) averaged 31.6% in majority-minority tracts (minority share 50% or more) versus 25.1% in other tracts in the same county: a gap of 6.5 points (median 5.9; volume-weighted 6.8; ratio about 1.28). Denial was higher in majority-minority tracts in 90% of counties. Equivalent approval rates are about 68% vs 75%.

Caveats: this is home-loan data, county stands in for metro (the table has no MSA field), and there are no controls for income, credit, or loan type.

Exclusions

The first query used the predicate tract_minority_population_percent IS NOT NULL. It removed zero tracts: 0 tracts in 2023 have a null minority share, and the result without the predicate is identical (31.6% vs 25.1%, gap 6.5 points, 525 counties). Counties with fewer than 200 decided applications in either tract group were left out for stability of the rate.

What This Report Does Not Answer

  • Small-business loan approval rate gap, majority-minority vs other tracts, same metro: No public dataset carries small-business applications/denials by tract. CRA and SBA data are originations/approvals only; CFPB Section 1071 data is not yet published. Delivered a labeled mortgage (HMDA) proxy at county grain instead.
