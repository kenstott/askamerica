Young Americans own homes at roughly 4-6 points lower rates than boomers and Gen X did at the same age

Redfin/IPUMS-CPS same-age generational comparison (2025); Census ACS B25007 (census.acs_housing_tenure_by_age), 2009-2024 5-year vintages

Summary

At the same age, today's young adults are about 4 to 6 percentage points less likely to own than earlier generations, or roughly 10 percent less likely in relative terms. Redfin's CPS analysis (2025): at 28, Gen Z owns at 38.3% versus 42.5% for Gen X and 44.4% for boomers (gap 4.2 and 6.1 points). At 36, millennials own at 57.2% versus 61.2% and 63.7% (gap 4.0 and 6.5 points). Millennials at 28 were at 36.8%, so Gen Z is slightly ahead of millennials.

Independent check from the warehouse (ACS)

Owner share of occupied units by householder age, national sum of states, 5-year ACS: ages 25-34 were 45.3% (2009), 37.6% (2016 low), 41.1% (2024). Ages 35-44: 65.9%, 57.9%, 60.9%. Ages 45-54: 74.2% in 2009 vs 69.6% in 2024. Under 25: 16.8% vs 16.2%. So ownership in the 25-44 bands has recovered from the mid-2010s trough but remains 4-5 points below 2009 (the 2009 vintage covers 2005-09, a housing-boom period). This corroborates a gap of about 4-5 points, but spans only 15 years.

Caveats

  • Redfin counts household heads only and excludes people living with parents or relatives; including them would make the population-wide gap larger, since young adults now head fewer households.
  • Redfin ages are single years from CPS; ACS uses 10-year bands and a rate over occupied units, not people. The two are not directly comparable.
  • I did not recompute the CPS figures; they come from Redfin's page. A different write-up (MPA Magazine) shows far lower figures on a different methodology and was not used.
  • Relative gap: 4.2/44.4 is about 9%, 6.5/63.7 about 10%.

What This Report Does Not Answer

  • Full generation-matched history in warehouse: Warehouse starts 2009; the pre-2009 boomer comparison comes only from Redfin.

Every query behind this report

1 warehouse call ran in this session, in order. Each is reproducible against the same snapshot.

query — 16 rows — 2189 ms
SELECT "year", SUM(owner_25_to_34) o2, SUM(renter_25_to_34) r2, SUM(owner_35_to_44) o3, SUM(renter_35_to_44) r3, SUM(owner_15_to_24) o1, SUM(renter_15_to_24) r1, SUM(owner_45_to_54) o4, SUM(renter_45_to_54) r4 FROM census.acs_housing_tenure_by_age WHERE geography='state' GROUP BY "year" ORDER BY "year"
