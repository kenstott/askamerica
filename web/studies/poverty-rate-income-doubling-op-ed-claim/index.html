The op-ed's claims about the 2023 poverty rate and household income since 2000 are very biased and false.

Article under reviewhttps://www.census.gov/library/publications/2024/demo/p60-283.html

Summary

False. Official 2023 poverty was 11.1% (SPM 12.9%), not 3.8%; the lowest state was 7.3%. Real median household income rose about 15% since 2000, not 100%. 'Twice as secure' is unsupported. The op-ed itself was not located; the source URL here is the Census report that tests it.
