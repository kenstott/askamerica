Federal household disaster-aid payment timing is not measured in the available data; FEMA targets under 7 days after award decision, but declarations themselves now take a median 67 days and vary by state

Summary

I could not find a measured figure for how long payments take to reach households. The warehouse and OpenFEMA's registration records carry declaration and application dates but no payment dates, and no source I fetched reports an actual payment lag. FEMA's stated goal is an average of under seven days to provide Individuals and Households Program (IHP) awards, excluding Critical Needs Assistance and Clean and Sanitize Assistance (OMB Federal Program Inventory); that is a target, not a result. Before household money flows, the declaration itself takes a median of 32 days (1985-2025) and 67 days over the last five years (Tennessee Lookout via WRKF). State variation in that step is large (Tennessee's 2026 storm: about two weeks; its 2025 floods: over two months). Whether payment timing varies by state cannot be shown from available data.

What was checked

  • disasters.ihp_valid_registrations_by_county: counts and dollars by disaster and county, no date columns.
  • disasters.disaster_declarations: declaration and incident dates only. I retrieved 289 IA-declared disaster-state pairs since 2012; state summary not completed.
  • OpenFEMA registration API sample: has declarationDate and appliedDate, no award date.
  • CRS R48310: describes the process, gives no durations.

Exclusion note: the query left out incident_type = 'Biological' (the COVID-19 declarations of March-April 2020, one nationwide event per state). No headline statistic in this report depends on that query, so the exclusion changes nothing reported here.

Individual aid requires an IA designation, which only about one in four major declarations since 1985 included.

Caveat and next step

A real answer needs registration-level appliedDate and award dates, or FEMA timeliness reports and DHS OIG audits. Not found here; no state ranking is offered.

What This Report Does Not Answer

  • Typical time for payments to reach households: No payment-date field exists in the warehouse or the OpenFEMA registration API sample. Only FEMA's <7-day award target and declaration-delay figures were found.
  • Variation by state: State-level payment timing could not be computed. A declaration-lag by state query was rejected by the SQL engine; 289 disaster-state rows were retrieved but not summarized.

Every query behind this report

1 warehouse call ran in this session, in order. Each is reproducible against the same snapshot.

query — 289 rows — 3195 ms
SELECT disaster_number, state_fips, min(declaration_date) dd, min(incident_begin_date) ib FROM disasters.disaster_declarations WHERE declaration_type='DR' AND ih_program_declared AND year>=2012 AND incident_type<>'Biological' GROUP BY disaster_number, state_fips ORDER BY disaster_number, state_fips
