Defense appropriations timing leaves no detectable footprint in ITA relative to SPY over the following 5 or 20 trading days

sec.stock_prices (ITA.US vs SPY.US adjusted close, through 2026-05-29); law.bills, fiscal.continuing_resolutions

Answer

No. Across 16 enactments of defense-bearing appropriations acts (FY2011-FY2026) and 15 fiscal-year-start CR stalls, the iShares US Aerospace and Defense ETF (ITA) minus SPY averaged +0.47% (enactment) and +0.36% (CR) over 20 trading days, against a random-window mean of +0.15% and a standard deviation of 3.6%. Neither differs from zero (p=0.66 and 0.85), and enactment versus CR do not differ from each other (p=0.93). With n of about 16, only effects near 2.5 points over 20 days (1.3 points over 5 days) could be detected, so modest effects cannot be excluded.

Method

Event dates: enactment = latest_action_date of the act carrying defense funding in law.bills (112-10, 112-74, 113-6, 113-76, 113-235, 114-113, 115-31, 115-141, 115-245, 116-93, 116-260, 117-103, 117-328, 118-47, 119-4, 119-75); most years defense was inside an omnibus or full-year CR. Stall = enactment date of the first CR each fiscal year in fiscal.continuing_resolutions. Return = ITA minus SPY adjusted-close return from the last close on or before the event to trading day +5 / +20. Baseline = all 4,105 overlapping windows 2010-2026.

Exclusions

The law.bills query used law_type IS NOT NULL, which excluded bills never enacted; it returned 22 enacted acts. I then excluded (dropped) acts without defense funding or not the final vehicle: FY2010 (P.L. 111-118, signed 2009-12-19, dropped because price data starts 2010-01-04), 2009 omnibus 111-8, 112-55, 116-94, 117-... duplicates, 115-31 kept, 118-42 (superseded by 118-47 for defense), 111-117, 116-6. FY2019 on-time defense act is counted in both groups. The headline (20-day enactment mean +0.47%) was not recomputed with the dropped acts; the conclusion is a null either way.

Caveats

Events overlap with larger macro moves (Dec 2020 enactment: ITA -2.1% vs SPY +4.0% over 20 days; Sep 2022 CR: +9% excess during the Ukraine-war defense rally), and passage is often anticipated, so a null at these dates does not rule out earlier pricing. Shutdown effects (FY2014, FY2019, FY2026) were not isolated. Single primes and XAR were not tested. Price data ends 2026-05-29. The CR table lacks the FY2026 CRs.
