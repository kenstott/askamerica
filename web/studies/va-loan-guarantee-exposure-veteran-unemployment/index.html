The warehouse has no county mortgage-delinquency series, so the question cannot be answered directly; the exposure side (VA share vs veteran unemployment) shows almost no relationship

HMDA LAR 2022 originations x ACS 2018-2022 B21005, counties with 200+ first-lien owner-occupied purchase loans and 500+ veteran labor force

Answer

I cannot say how closely delinquency tracks veteran unemployment: the warehouse holds no county-level mortgage delinquency (or foreclosure) data, and none split by VA loans. The only delinquency series is national (NY Fed). What I could measure is the exposure side. Across 1,493 counties, VA share of 2022 purchase originations has essentially zero correlation with county veteran unemployment (r = -0.01). In the 373 highest-VA-share counties (12.4%+) r = -0.01; in the 146 counties with 20%+ VA share r = +0.20. Veteran unemployment there is dispersed (median 3.2%, 10th-90th percentile 1.0% to 6.9%), partly small-sample noise.

Exclusions

Counties excluded: those with fewer than 200 qualifying 2022 originations or fewer than 500 veterans in the labor force, and any county with a null county FIPS in the ACS table (the county_fips IS NOT NULL predicate left out state-level rows). I did not run the statistic without these filters, so the effect of the exclusions on r is not measured; they mostly drop small rural counties where veteran unemployment is noisiest.

  • VA share of originations is a proxy for exposure, not share of loans outstanding.
  • Not a delinquency result; cross-section, one vintage, no controls (age mix, base counties, prices).
  • To close the gap, county delinquency (e.g. CFPB Mortgage Performance Trends or a VA/MBA servicer file) is needed; not pulled.

What This Report Does Not Answer

  • County mortgage delinquency: No county-grain mortgage delinquency series exists in the catalog; not substituted.
