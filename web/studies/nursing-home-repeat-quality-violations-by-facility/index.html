Illinois leads the nation in serious (G-L) nursing home citations since Nov 2023; the top 12 homes are all still CMS-certified

CMS Provider Data Catalog health deficiencies (r5ix-sfxw) and provider information (4pq5-n9py); citations with survey date 2023-11-19 or later

Answer

The question names no state, so I measured every state. Illinois has the most actual-harm or immediate-jeopardy (scope-severity G-L) citations per home: 2,664 citations across 558 of its 664 homes, or 401 per 100 homes. Rhode Island is next at 282 and Vermont at 227. Texas ranks sixth at 199 per 100 homes, but it leads on immediate-jeopardy (J-L) citations alone, with 1,881 against Illinois's 452. I picked Illinois on the broader G-L measure.

Top Illinois homes by G-L citations (J-L in parentheses): Nexus Pavilion at Belleville 26 (4), Nexus at Elmwood 24 (5), Nexus at Alton 22 (1), Metropolis Rehab 20 (3), Generations at Applewood 19 (1), Bria of Cahokia 17 (2), Nexus at Palos 17 (2), Bria of Woodriver 17 (7), Archer Heights Healthcare 17 (3), Goldwater Care Peoria Heights 16 (6), Evercare at University 15 (3), La Bella of Woodstock 15 (3). Eleven are rated 1 star and one 2 stars; fines run from $211K to $991K each.

Are they still operating?

All 12 appear in CMS's current certified-provider file, and none has a termination date in the CMS Provider of Services termination table. Most were surveyed again in 2026. That shows current certification, not proof the doors are open, and I did not check Illinois Department of Public Health licensing. Bria of Cahokia (last survey 2025-09) and Archer Heights (2025-05) are the ones to verify first.

Caveats

  • Window is survey dates from 2023-11-19; citations, not distinct incidents.
  • Fines and stars are current snapshot values.
  • Small states have noisier per-home rates.

Every query behind this report

2 warehouse calls ran in this session, in order. Each is reproducible against the same snapshot.

query — 8 rows — 2651 ms
SELECT d.state, COUNT(*) AS gl_cites, COUNT(DISTINCT d.ccn) AS homes_cited, COUNT(*) FILTER (WHERE d.scope_severity_code IN ('J','K','L')) AS ij_cites, MAX(n.homes) AS homes_total, ROUND(100.0*COUNT(*)/MAX(n.homes),1) AS per100 FROM health.cms_nursing_home_deficiencies d JOIN (SELECT state, COUNT(*) AS homes FROM health.cms_nursing_home GROUP BY state) n ON n.state=d.state WHERE d.scope_severity_code IN ('G','H','I','J','K','L') AND d.survey_date >= '2023-11-19' GROUP BY d.state ORDER BY per100 DESC FETCH FIRST 8 ROWS ONLY
query — 12 rows — 3973 ms
SELECT d.ccn, d.provider_name, d.city, COUNT(*) AS gl, COUNT(*) FILTER (WHERE d.scope_severity_code IN ('J','K','L')) AS ij, MAX(d.survey_date) AS last_survey, MAX(n.overall_rating) AS stars, MAX(n.total_fines_dollars) AS fines, MAX(n.certified_beds) AS beds, MAX(t.termination_date) AS term_date, MAX(t.termination_code) AS term_code FROM health.cms_nursing_home_deficiencies d LEFT JOIN health.cms_nursing_home n ON n.ccn=d.ccn LEFT JOIN health.cms_pos_termination_history t ON t.provider_number=d.ccn WHERE d.state='IL' AND d.scope_severity_code IN ('G','H','I','J','K','L') AND d.survey_date >= '2023-11-19' GROUP BY d.ccn, d.provider_name, d.city ORDER BY gl DESC FETCH FIRST 12 ROWS ONLY
