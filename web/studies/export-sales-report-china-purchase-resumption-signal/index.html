China's pauses and resumptions show up in USDA weekly soybean data within one to three weeks, as swings of 2 to 6 times a normal week

Summary

The weekly USDA export-sales report (ag.fas_export_sales, China = code 5700) reacts fast. Net sales are the leading signal and show the first reported week after buying starts; physical shipments lag by about 5 weeks. Resumption weeks print 2 to 6 times a normal peak-season week (normal: mean about 0.65-0.7 MMT, sd about 0.4-0.6 MMT, Sep-Feb 2020-24).

Resume: Oct-Dec 2025

China soybean net sales were about 0 through Sep-Oct 2025 (all-commodity weekly sales 1-6 thousand t). Week ending 2025-10-30: 232,000 t, then 232,000 (Nov 6), -100,000 (Nov 13), 2,142,000 (Nov 20), 509,000, 1.01 MMT, 1.38 MMT (Dec 11). Shipments (weekly_exports) stayed 0 until Dec 4 (71 thousand t), 202 thousand Dec 11, 386 thousand Dec 18, 0.9 MMT Jan 8. So sales appear within about a week of the signal; the big print came about 3 weeks after; shipments about 5 weeks later. The 2.14 MMT week is about 3x the normal mean and roughly +3 sd. Note: reports for late Sept to Oct 2025 are thin in the table (shutdown period).

Pause: 2018

Net sales turned persistently negative (cancellations) from late June 2018: -366,000 t (Jun 28), then -120,000 (Jul 26) and runs of -60,000 to -195,000 through Nov, with shipments at 0 from Sep. In 2010-17 the normal Sep-Feb median net sale was 0.3-0.9 MMT per week; 2018 median was -2,750 t. A pause appears as zero or negative weeks, not as a single spike. Resume: Dec 13, 2018 +1.56 MMT, Feb 14, 2019 +3.92 MMT (about 6x normal mean, +4.5 sd, the table maximum).

Caveats

Soybeans only for the detail; wheat and sorghum are small and episodic (sorghum 0.1-0.5 MMT per week in 2026). Net sales can include flash-reported round figures and later reversals (-807,000 on Jan 3, 2019; -136,000 on Sep 3, 2026), so single weeks overstate durable demand. Policy dates were not fetched. Data checked through 2026-11-11.

What This Report Does Not Answer

  • Policy event dates are from memory/news, not queried; Oct 2025 weeks affected by the government shutdown gap: Event dates (Oct 30, 2025 Trump-Xi meeting; Dec 2018 truce; Jan 2020 Phase One) not verified by fetch; lag estimates are approximate.
