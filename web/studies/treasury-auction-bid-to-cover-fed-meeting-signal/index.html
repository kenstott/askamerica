Weak Treasury auctions just before an FOMC meeting show no link to how the meeting turns out for rates (2022-2026)

Treasury Fiscal Data auctions_query (2/5/7/10-yr notes); Fed FOMC calendar; econ.fred_indicators DFF and DGS2. Jan 2022 to Oct 2026.

Summary

No. Across 39 FOMC decisions (Jan 2022 to Oct 2026), the 19 that had a 2/5/7/10-year note auction in the prior 7 days show essentially zero relationship between auction weakness (bid-to-cover z-score) and the outcome: Spearman rho -0.10 vs hike/hold/cut (p=0.68) and r -0.08 vs the 2-year yield move on decision day (p=0.73). Tests with a 10-day window or a trailing-12 baseline are equally flat (|r| at most 0.15, all p above 0.35). This is a small sample, so it rules out a strong link, not a weak one.

Method

Auction bid-to-cover from Treasury Fiscal Data (2-, 5-, 7-year notes at their regular cycle, plus 10-year; 2-year FRN rows were excluded because they carry no fixed yield). Each auction was z-scored against its own tenor, both over the full sample and against the prior 12 auctions of the same tenor; the meeting's score is the mean z of auctions in the 7 (or 10) days before the decision date. Outcome: decision direction from the effective fed funds rate change across the meeting (6 hikes, 11 holds, 2 cuts in the 19 matched meetings), and the 2-year Treasury yield change on decision day versus the prior day (warehouse DGS2). Meeting dates were read from the Fed's FOMC calendar page.

Exclusions: the warehouse query dropped only calendar days where FRED has no value (weekends, holidays, where value IS NOT NULL); all 39 meetings still returned both a before and after reading, so the result is the same with or without that filter. Separately, 20 meetings were left out of the correlation because no coupon auction fell in the prior 7 days (coupon auctions cluster late in the month; FOMC decisions mostly fall mid-month).

Results

  • Lowest-tercile (weakest) auction weeks: mean 2-year move -4.1bp vs -0.5bp for the rest (7-day, full z); hikes in 3 of 7 weak-auction meetings vs 3 of 12 others; no cuts after weak auctions. These differences are within noise at n=7.
  • Mean z by decision: cut 0.36, hold 0.12, hike 0.27 - no ordering.
  • Split by era: 2022-23 r=-0.13 (n=8); 2024+ r=0.08 (n=11).
  • Sign check: a weak auction would be expected to pair with higher yields; the weak-tercile 2-year move was slightly negative, the opposite sign, and tiny.

Caveats

Small n; the policy path in 2022-23 (a hiking cycle largely pre-signalled by the Fed) dominates the decision outcomes and was set by inflation data, not auction demand; decisions are mostly priced in beforehand, so the meeting-day yield move is a surprise measure only. Bid-to-cover is a demand proxy; the tail versus when-issued yield and bills, 20- and 30-year auctions were not tested. Full-sample z-scores use hindsight; the trailing version avoids that and gives the same answer. The warehouse holds no auction results table; auction data came from the Treasury Fiscal Data API read directly.

What This Report Does Not Answer

  • Tail / when-issued yield: not in the Fiscal Data auction fields pulled; bid-to-cover used instead. Bills, 20y and 30y not included.

WITH m AS (SELECT CAST(d AS DATE) md FROM (VALUES ('2022-01-26'),('2022-03-16'),('2022-05-04'),('2022-06-15'),('2022-07-27'),('2022-09-21'),('2022-11-02'),('2022-12-14'),('2023-02-01'),('2023-03-22'),('2023-05-03'),('2023-06-14'),('2023-07-26'),('2023-09-20'),('2023-11-01'),('2023-12-13'),('2024-01-31'),('2024-03-20'),('2024-05-01'),('2024-06-12'),('2024-07-31'),('2024-09-18'),('2024-11-07'),('2024-12-18'),('2025-01-29'),('2025-03-19'),('2025-05-07'),('2025-06-18'),('2025-07-30'),('2025-09-17'),('2025-10-29'),('2025-12-10'),('2026-01-28'),('2026-03-18'),('2026-04-29'),('2026-06-17'),('2026-07-29'),('2026-09-16'),('2026-10-28')) t(d)),
f AS (SELECT CAST("date" AS DATE) dt, series, value FROM econ.fred_indicators WHERE series IN ('DFF','DGS2') AND "date" >= '2021-12-01' AND value IS NOT NULL)
SELECT m.md,
 MAX(CASE WHEN f.series='DFF' AND f.dt = m.md - INTERVAL '1' DAY THEN f.value END) dff_pre,
 MAX(CASE WHEN f.series='DFF' AND f.dt = m.md + INTERVAL '3' DAY THEN f.value END) dff_post,
 MAX(CASE WHEN f.series='DGS2' AND f.dt = m.md - INTERVAL '1' DAY THEN f.value END) y2_pre,
 MAX(CASE WHEN f.series='DGS2' AND f.dt = m.md THEN f.value END) y2_day
FROM m JOIN f ON f.dt BETWEEN m.md - INTERVAL '1' DAY AND m.md + INTERVAL '3' DAY
GROUP BY m.md ORDER BY m.md
