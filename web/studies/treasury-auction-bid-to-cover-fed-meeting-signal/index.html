Weak Treasury auctions in the week before an FOMC meeting showed no relationship to the meeting's rate outcome, 2016-2025

fiscal.treasury_auction_results + econ.fred_indicators (DGS2, DGS10, DFF); 75 scheduled FOMC meetings, 2016-2025

Answer

No. Across 75 scheduled FOMC meetings (2016-2025), a weak note or bond auction in the preceding week did not line up with the meeting's rate outcome. Correlation between the weakest auction's bid-to-cover z-score and the 2-year yield move on decision day is r = -0.03. The mean 2-year move was -1.55bp after weak-auction weeks and -1.55bp after other weeks (Welch p = 0.998). Hike, cut and hold frequencies were about the same in both groups. The sample is small (20 weak weeks), so a modest effect cannot be excluded, but nothing visible here supports the idea.

Method

Weakness: for each nominal note/bond auction (TIPS and FRNs excluded), bid-to-cover minus the mean of the prior 8 auctions of the same original term, divided by their standard deviation (at least 4 prior auctions required). Per meeting I took the minimum z across coupon auctions held in the 7 days before decision day. 'Weak' = minimum z of -1 or lower (20 meetings). Outcomes: change in DGS2 and DGS10 from the prior trading day to decision day; and hike/cut/hold from the effective funds rate (DFF) averaged 2-5 days after versus 2-5 days before, with a move of 15bp or more counted as a change.

Other correlations with the minimum z: 10-year move r = -0.02; funds-rate change r = +0.03; absolute 2-year move r = +0.01. No dose-response pattern appears across buckets.

What was excluded

Excluded rows: auctions with a null bid_to_cover_ratio (unsettled 2026 auctions, outside the test window) and dates with null y2 or y10 (holidays, handled by using the prior trading day). The minz IS NOT NULL filter dropped four of the 79 meetings (2016-01-27, 2016-03-16, 2016-09-21, 2018-03-21) that had no coupon auction with enough history in the 7-day window; the headline uses the remaining 75. Those four cannot be scored with the weakness measure, so there is no with-exclusion figure; their 2-year moves were -0.01, -0.11, 0.00 and -0.03, which would not change the null.

Caveats

  • Meeting dates 2021-2025 were read from federalreserve.gov/monetarypolicy/fomccalendars.htm. Dates for 2016-2020 were typed from my own recollection and only sanity-checked against funds-rate changes; they were not fetched from the Fed. The 2020 emergency actions (March 2020) and the 2025-08-22 notation vote (a framework statement, not a rate decision) are left out. 2026 meetings are left out because the series is incomplete.
  • Early-2016 z-scores lack 2012-2015 history because those years are not loaded.
  • Bid-to-cover is a demand proxy, not the 'tail' traders watch (high yield versus when-issued), which is unavailable here. Weak demand may show up in tails or indirect-bidder share instead; not tested.
  • Policy is mostly set by data and guidance, and the auction calendar is mechanical. Hike and cut counts per group are tiny, so those percentages are noisy, and the rate cycle is not controlled for.
  • Decision-day yield moves mix the Fed's own news with earlier-priced auction information; a real link would more likely show in pre-meeting yield drift, which was not tested.

What This Report Does Not Answer

  • Tail (high yield vs when-issued) measure: When-issued yields are not in the warehouse; bid-to-cover z-score used instead
  • Full-history coverage: Warehouse holds auctions only for 2010-2011 and 2016-2026; 2012-2015 absent, so the test uses 2016-2025
