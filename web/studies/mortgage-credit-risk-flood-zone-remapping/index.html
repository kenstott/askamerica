The warehouse cannot say how much mortgage debt sits in newly flood-mapped properties or at which lenders; no public dataset joins the three pieces

HMDA 2018-2025 (CFPB), FEMA/NFIP tables; web literature as of 2026-11-22

Answer

I cannot give a dollar figure or a lender ranking. Answering would need three things joined at the property level: (1) which properties were newly mapped into a Special Flood Hazard Area (SFHA) by a map revision, (2) the outstanding balance of the mortgage on each, and (3) the lender or servicer holding it. The warehouse has none of (1), and the public data lacks (2) and (3) in usable form.

What was checked

  • Flood map revisions: no table carries FEMA's flood map layer (NFHL), LOMRs or map-revision history. The catalog search returned no match for NFHL. disasters.nfip_policies and nfip_claims carry a flood_zone label per policy or claim, which is not the same as a property newly mapped in.
  • Debt balances: HMDA (housing.hmda_lar, 136.6M loan records, 2018-2025) records loan amounts at origination, not balances outstanding. It has census tract and the reporting institution's LEI, but no flood-zone flag. It cannot tell you which loans fall in newly mapped areas.
  • Lender concentration: HMDA identifies the originator, not the current servicer or holder. Many loans are sold to Fannie Mae, Freddie Mac or Ginnie Mae, so originator shares would not describe who holds the debt.

Context bound only, not an answer: HMDA originations nationwide were about $2.0 trillion in 2024 and $2.4 trillion in 2025 (CFPB aggregates, housing.hmda_loans, action_taken=1). The newly mapped share of that is unknown.

What the literature says

I found no study that totals outstanding mortgage debt on newly mapped properties. Related work: a Federal Reserve paper on the 2016 Orleans Parish remapping finds lenders price the change (about 6 basis points higher rates on properties removed from the SFHA, per the search summary; I did not read the full text). A New York Fed staff report finds lenders treat unmapped flood risk differently, with weaker effects for non-banks and small local banks. A CFPB 2025 report notes exposure estimates vary widely by flood-risk measure.

What would be needed

Overlay FEMA NFHL effective-date polygons (new SFHA additions) on geocoded loan-level mortgage data with servicer identity, such as a credit bureau or servicer tape. A feasible partial version: tract-level HMDA origination dollars in tracts overlapping new SFHA areas, by originator LEI. That needs NFHL tract overlays, which are not loaded. I have logged this as a sourcing gap.

What This Report Does Not Answer

  • Dollar amount of outstanding mortgage debt in newly mapped flood-zone properties: No flood map revision data and no outstanding-balance data in the warehouse; only origination context given.
  • Lender concentration: HMDA gives originator not servicer, and no flood-zone flag, so concentration cannot be computed.
