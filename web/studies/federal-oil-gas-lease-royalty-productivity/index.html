Federal onshore oil and gas leasing returned about $331 royalty dollars per leased acre in FY2024, but states ranged from $0 to $1,339

Summary

In FY2024, federal onshore oil, gas and NGL royalties were $7.30B on 22.0M leased acres: about $331 per leased acre and $588 per producing acre (12.4M). The spread is huge. Across 27 royalty-paying states the median is about $27 per leased acre (quartiles $4 to $76). New Mexico alone is $1,339 per leased acre ($5.56B, 76% of the total); without New Mexico the national figure is $97. Over time the national figure ranged from $47 (FY2004) to $364 (FY2023); 2008 $85, 2016 $54, 2021 $151.

Method and exclusions

Royalties: lands.onrr_revenues, land_class Federal, Onshore, revenue_type Royalties, commodities Oil, Gas, NGL (bonus, rents, other revenue and offshore excluded by design). Acres: lands.blm_oil_gas_acreage (BLM leased and producing acres, federal only), joined on state and fiscal year. The predicate state_name IS NOT NULL removed zero rows for FY2024 (verified: no onshore federal royalty rows have a null state), so all 27 royalty-paying states are covered. Per producing acre: NM $1,462, CA $1,000, ND $767, WY $202, AK $627 (most Alaska leases are not producing).

Caveats: descriptive only, no price control; the time swing mostly tracks oil prices and Permian output. Acreage is year-end, revenue is fiscal-year flow. Leased acreage fell from 44M (2008) to 22M, which also lifts the per-acre figure. Offshore royalties ($6.2B oil in FY2024) are outside scope.

What This Report Does Not Answer

  • Formal significance test / price control: Descriptive only; oil price drives the time variation and was not modeled.
