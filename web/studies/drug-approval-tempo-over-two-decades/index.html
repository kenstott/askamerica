Median FDA review time for new drugs has been roughly flat at 11-12 months for twenty years; the big drop came before 2005

Published sources (FDA PDUFA FY2025 report, Pink Sheet, Tufts CSDD, ISPOR); warehouse cannot compute review time

Summary

Over the last twenty years the FDA's median time to approve a new drug has barely moved: about 11 to 12 months for novel drugs in 2014-2016, 2022, 2023 and 2024 (11.7 months in 2024). Standard reviews were about 12-13 months in the mid-2000s and about 12 months in recent FDA data; priority reviews run about 6-8 months. The big drop (26.6 months pre-1993 to about 10 months) came with PDUFA, before 2005. Total development time (IND to approval) did not shrink: Tufts CSDD puts it at 101.5 months for 2014-2018, up 5% from 2008-2013.

Evidence

  • FDA PDUFA FY2025 report (read): median time to approval for receipt cohorts FY2016-FY2025 is about 8 months for priority and roughly 11.7-12.0 for standard, with an FY2023 bump due to NME three-month extensions.
  • Pink Sheet (read): 2024 median 11.7 months for novel approvals (CDER 11.8, CBER 10.6); 2023 CDER 11 months, matching 2022.
  • Pink Sheet via search (page body not retrievable): CDER novel median 11 months in 2014-2016; 2004-2007 FDA figures 6.0 (priority) and 12.9 (standard), from an FDA archive page that failed to load, so treat as search-summarized.
  • ISPOR 2025 poster (read): median 26.6 months pre-PDUFA vs 10.0 months in the FDASIA-2024 period.
  • Tufts CSDD (read): regulatory phase 11.7 months in 2014-2018, down 1.9 months (14%) from 2008-2013; clinical phase 89.8 months, up 8.1%; total 101.5 months.
  • Warehouse: health.fda_drug_approvals holds only approval dates (e.g., 74 NDAs in 2005, 91 in 2024, 110 in 2025) with no submission date, so review time cannot be computed from it.

Caveats

I did not obtain a clean year-by-year 2005-2021 series. Sources mix medians and means, CDER-only and all-application scopes, and fiscal vs calendar years. Approval counts are not review times. Priority review now covers about half of approvals, so a flat overall median can hide faster priority reviews. Review time is a small share of the roughly 8.5-year development path.

What This Report Does Not Answer

  • Complete year-by-year 2005-2024 series: Not found in sources read; warehouse has no submission dates; used selected years.
