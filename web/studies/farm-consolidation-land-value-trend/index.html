Farmland consolidation is real but concentrated in the largest operations, and it has only loosely tracked land values

USDA Census of Agriculture (Table 1, Table 9), ERS farm real estate values, NASS state series 2009-2025 (warehouse). Operations, not owners.

Summary

Over 1982-2022 farms fell about 15% (2.24M to 1.90M) and land in farms 11% (986.8M to 880.1M acres), but average farm size rose only from about 440 to 463 acres. Consolidation is at the top: the share of cropland on farms of 2,000+ acres rose from 15% (1987) to 41% (2017) per USDA ERS, and in 2022 farms of 5,000+ acres (2% of farms) held 42% of farm acres. Land values rose strongly (nominal $823/acre in 1982, $3,846 Census 2022, $4,350 in 2025; real about +79% 1982-2022) but not in lockstep: real values fell 37% from 1982 to 1987 while consolidation continued, and in a 48-state panel for 2009-2023 land value does not predict farm size (coefficient 0.037, p=0.37). Caveat: these data are about operators, not owners; much cropland is rented, and I found no 40-year owner-concentration series.

From 2002 Census coverage of small farms improved, so 1997-2002 changes are not comparable. The count of 1,000+ acre farms has been flat near 173,000; the shift is how much land the largest hold (1,000+ acre farms: about 71% of farm acres in 2017, 73% in 2022).

Has it tracked land values?

Across 7 Census years real value and average size correlate -0.55 (+0.46 from 2002), uninformative with n=7 trending series. Warehouse panel (48 states, AK and HI excluded, 2009-2023, state and year fixed effects, state-clustered): log farm size on log land value = 0.037 (SE 0.041, p=0.37, n=720). Cross-state 2012-2022 change in size vs change in value: r=-0.06 (n=48). Not controlled: interest rates, technology, rental expansion, commodity prices.

Limits

  • Operators, not owners; no 1982 size-class split retrieved.
  • Warehouse tables start 2009; the long series is from Census/ERS documents fetched.
  • The 41% (2017) ERS share came from a search summary; 1982 farm count (about 2.24M) is from memory, the 1982 acreage from a USDA fact sheet via search.

What This Report Does Not Answer

  • Ownership specifically: Census size data are operators, not owners; no owner-concentration series fetched.
