Port import volumes do not reliably lead retail inventory build-ups: the implied lead is 0-3 months and rests on the 2020-22 shock

Summary

There is no stable, measurable lead. In the one window with real port data (BTS container TEUs, Jan 2019 - Oct 2022), import volume correlates most strongly with retail inventory changes at lag 0-1 months (r about 0.66 / 0.62), fading to about 0.3-0.4 beyond 3 months. That is a short lead of roughly 0-2 months and it is driven by the 2020-22 pandemic V-shape. With the longer customs-value proxy (2014-2025), the sign and lag flip across sub-periods (2014-19: slightly negative at short lags; 2023-25: strongly negative at 6-10 months), so no regime-independent lead exists in this data.

What was computed

  • Retail inventories: FRED RETAILIMSA (Census, seasonally adjusted), 2013-2025 months.
  • Port volumes: transport.bts_port_teu, 9 ports, imports plus exports combined, 46 months only (source stopped Oct 2022). Correlated 3-month average TEU with the 3-month change in inventories: r=0.66 at lag 0, 0.62 lag 1, 0.52 lag 2, 0.41 lag 3. Total TEU level vs inventory level peaks flat (0.55-0.62) at all lags, i.e. shared trend, not a lead.
  • Long proxy: econ.trade_imports total customs value (country rows only, excluding HS 27 energy), not seasonally adjusted, so I used 12-month log growth of both series. Full 2014-25 correlations rise monotonically to 0.51 at lag 12, a classic shared-trend and overlapping-window artifact, not a lead. Sub-periods disagree (see chart).

Caveats

  • Customs value is dollars at all ports and goods (not TEUs, not retail goods only); inventories include autos and are in dollars. Prices muddy both.
  • TEU data includes exports and covers only top ports; n is about 43 with strong autocorrelation, so no meaningful significance; I did not run a leave-one-out or Granger test.
  • Correlation is not predictive lead: contemporaneous sign at lag 0 being highest means inventories and imports co-moved, not that imports led.
  • NRF/Hackett Port Tracker, which forecasts import lead into inventories (often cited as roughly 1-3 months transit plus processing), is not available as a bulk series; I did not verify it externally.
