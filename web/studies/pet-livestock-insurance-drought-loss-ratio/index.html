Livestock drought payouts rose roughly 4-5x in 2021-2025 versus 2017-2019, and about half sits in five states

USDA RMA Summary of Business (ag.rma_crop_insurance), USDA FSA payments (ag.fsa_commodity_payments); nominal USD; 2017-2025

Summary

Livestock-related drought payouts grew sharply. Pasture, Rangeland and Forage (PRF) rainfall-index insurance indemnities averaged $1.19B a year in 2021-2025 against $0.32B in 2017-2019 (3.8x; $1.49B in 2025). FSA livestock disaster payments (Livestock Forage Disaster Program, ELAP, Livestock Indemnity, Emergency Livestock Relief) averaged $2.19B a year against $0.43B (5.2x), peaking near $3.5B in 2023. Both are concentrated: the top five states take about 52% of PRF indemnity (TX, FL, NV, AZ, NM) and about 46.5% of FSA livestock payments (TX, OK, CA, MO, NE). Texas leads both.

Numbers

  • PRF indemnity 2021-25 total $5.93B. Top states: TX $1.12B, FL $0.55B, NV $0.53B, AZ $0.50B, NM $0.38B, OR $0.36B, CA $0.31B.
  • FSA livestock programs 2021-25 total $10.97B. Top states: TX $1.47B, OK $1.42B, CA $0.92B, MO $0.66B, NE $0.63B, KS $0.62B, MT $0.60B. Nebraska went from about $6M/yr to $127M/yr; Texas from $46M/yr to $294M/yr.
  • FSA growth came in waves: LFP spiked in 2014 ($4.4B) and 2022-23 (about $1.9B and $1.8B); ELRP ($0.6B, $1.4B, $1.0B in 2022, 2023, 2025) and ELAP (up to $0.66B in 2025) are new large pieces.

Caveats

Data-quality note: an exploratory query on ag.rma_crop_insurance flagged rollup_contamination in commodity_name (an 'All Other Crops' rollup alongside leaf crops). All reported PRF figures filter to the single leaf value 'Pasture,Rangeland,Forage', so none double-counts a rollup.

The 2017-2019 baseline is a choice. Dollars are nominal; PRF liability and premium also grew about 3x as coverage expanded, so part of indemnity growth is participation, not only worse drought. FSA year is the disbursement year, and ELRP and ELAP cover wildfire and other losses as well as drought; I did not isolate drought-only payments, and the cause-of-loss attribution query failed. The PRF series has no 2015 row and begins in 2014 here. RMA Livestock Risk Protection and Livestock Gross Margin are not in this extract. FEMA aid was excluded because drought is rarely FEMA-declared. Descriptive only; no significance or leave-one-out testing.

What This Report Does Not Answer

  • drought-only attribution: Cause-of-loss query failed; programs are livestock-related, not strictly drought-only.
