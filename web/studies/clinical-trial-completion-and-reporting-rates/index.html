About 3 in 10 completed interventional trials post results on ClinicalTrials.gov, ranging from about 50% for industry to under 5% for non-US government and hospital sponsors

Summary

Among 137,671 interventional trials registered on ClinicalTrials.gov with primary completion 2010-2023 (health.clinical_trials, updated since 2020), 70% (96,987) are marked COMPLETED. Only 29.5% of completed trials have posted results. Terminated trials post at 47%. Roughly 25% of all trials in the window have results.

By funder: NIH 79%, FDA-linked FED (e.g., VA) 69%, industry 50%, other (mostly universities/hospitals) 21%, other government 4.5%. Named sponsors range from 96% (GlaxoSmithKline, VA ORD) to 0% (Riphah International University) among sponsors with 300+ completed trials: Pfizer 84%, Lilly 81%, Novartis 59%, Janssen 34%, Mayo 44%, Cairo University 1.1%, AP-HP 1.3%.

Caveats: results posting is legally required only for FDAAA-applicable trials (drugs/devices, US nexus), and the table has no applicable flag, so low rates for non-US academic sponsors partly reflect no legal obligation. The table covers studies updated since 2020; statuses are sponsor-reported, and 21,469 trials are stuck in UNKNOWN status with almost no results.

What This Report Does Not Answer

  • Applicable-trial (FDAAA) legal denominator: Registry has no applicable-clinical-trial flag; used all interventional trials as a proxy, so rates understate compliance among legally covered trials.
