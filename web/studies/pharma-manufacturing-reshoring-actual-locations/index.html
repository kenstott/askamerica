Pharma plants are physically going up in North Carolina, Indiana, Pennsylvania/Ohio and Virginia; most of the $370-500B pledged is still unbuilt

BLS QCEW NAICS 3254 private, 2022 vs 2025 (econ.county_qcew); groundbreaking press releases through Nov 2026; UNC Kenan-Flagler tracker

Summary

Construction is verifiably underway in a few clusters: North Carolina (Holly Springs, Clayton, Wilson, Sanford, Durham), Indiana (Lilly Lebanon and Indianapolis, Roche Indianapolis), Pennsylvania (Lilly Lehigh Valley $3.5B, broke ground Nov 12 2026; GSK Marietta), Virginia (Lilly Goochland $5B, broke ground Oct 22 2026, the one site combining API and finished product), plus Ohio, Illinois, Delaware, Oregon. Pledges total $370-480B+ over 4-10 years, but no tracker states what share has broken ground; treat most pledges as unbuilt.

Top counties by QCEW private pharma (NAICS 3254) job growth 2022 to 2025: Union NJ +3,295; Marion IN +1,498; Montgomery MD +1,007; Lake IL +963; Montgomery PA +934; Franklin OH +758; Durham NC +681; Miami-Dade FL +679; Greenville SC +674; Wake NC +582. These are operating jobs at existing plants, not construction.

Exclusions: a.annual_avg_emplvl IS NOT NULL and b.annual_avg_emplvl IS NOT NULL

The predicates a.annual_avg_emplvl IS NOT NULL and b.annual_avg_emplvl IS NOT NULL excluded 15 counties because BLS suppressed their employment (NULL, disclosure code N): 40111 Okmulgee OK, 42055 Franklin PA, 41043 Linn OR, 42089 Monroe PA, 41033 Josephine OR, 42087 Mifflin PA, 40119 Payne OK, 42097 Northumberland PA, 42041 Cumberland PA, 42043 Dauphin PA, 42005 Armstrong PA, 42011 Berks PA, 41047 Marion OR, 42075 Lebanon PA, 42125 Washington PA. Headline with the exclusion: Union NJ +3,295 leads. Without the exclusion the change cannot be computed (NULL, not zero); their establishment counts are only 1 to 5 each (Monroe PA 3 to 5, Dauphin PA 1 to 3, Lebanon PA 2 to 3), so none plausibly alters the top 12. Single-plant counties Boone IN, Cabarrus NC, Johnston NC and Goochland VA are also suppressed; Boone IN establishments rose 2 to 4.

Construction evidence

UNC Kenan-Flagler list of broken-ground sites (non-exhaustive, 17): Novo Nordisk Clayton NC $4.1B; Fujifilm Holly Springs $3.2B; Amgen Holly Springs $1.5B; Genentech Holly Springs $700M; J&J Wilson NC $2B; Kyowa Kirin Sanford NC; Lilly Indianapolis and Lebanon IN $4.5B each; Roche Indianapolis $550M; GSK Marietta PA $800M; Merck Wilmington DE $1B; Vetter Des Plaines IL; AbbVie North Chicago IL; Hikma Columbus OH.

2026 groundbreakings: Lilly Lehigh Valley PA, Lilly Goochland VA, Lonza Bend OR (Sept 14, due 2029). Announced only: Bayer New Albany OH $2.2B; Novartis Denton TX.

Caveats

Web figures come from press releases and search summaries; I read the UNC and Think Global Health pages but not every company release. Think Global Health (Nov 2025) notes unclear greenfield vs brownfield shares and weak equipment-order signals. API-only capacity is not separable in NAICS 3254.

What This Report Does Not Answer

  • Exhaustive enumeration of all sites: No complete national construction registry exists; lists are non-exhaustive; QCEW suppresses single-plant counties.
