Pharma plants are actually under construction in Texas, Pennsylvania, Indiana, North Carolina, Ohio and Oregon; completed hiring so far shows up in NJ, Indiana, Maryland and NC

BLS QCEW NAICS 3254 private employment by county, 2022 vs 2025; company releases and trade press through Nov 2026

Answer

The announced total (over $370B in pledges per DPR; over $480B per a Nov 2025 Think Global Health tally) far exceeds what has been confirmed as broken ground. Confirmed construction is concentrated in a handful of clusters: Indiana (Lilly Lebanon and Indianapolis, Roche Indianapolis), North Carolina (Novo Nordisk Clayton, Amgen/Genentech/Fujifilm Holly Springs, J&J Wilson, Kyowa Kirin Sanford), Texas (Lilly Houston, $6.5B, ground broken Sept 21, 2026; Novartis Denton, May 2026), Pennsylvania (Lilly Lehigh Valley, $3.5B, ground broken Nov 12, 2026; GSK Marietta), plus Illinois, Ohio, Delaware, Virginia and Oregon (Genentech Hillsboro, $750M, Sept 28, 2026). Several 2026 announcements are still only announced: Merck Montgomery County PA ($2.9B), Bayer New Albany OH ($2.2B, construction mid-2027), GSK Upper Merion PA (over $800M, construction 2027), Novartis Morrisville NC API.

Evidence of actually built (warehouse)

I ranked counties by change in private NAICS 3254 (pharmaceutical and medicine manufacturing) employment, 2022 to 2025, from econ.county_qcew. Largest gains: Union NJ +3,295 (3,758 to 7,053), Marion IN +1,498, Montgomery MD +1,007, Lake IL +963, Montgomery PA +934, Franklin OH +758, Durham NC +681, Wake NC +582 (1,101 to 1,683). Harris TX is flat to down (2,189 to 2,088): Houston is construction, not yet operations.

Excluded units: the ranking query dropped every county whose 2022 or 2025 employment is NULL (predicates j25 IS NOT NULL, j22 IS NOT NULL). Those are BLS-suppressed single-employer cells, not zeros, and they include Boone IN (Lebanon, Lilly), Johnston, Harnett and Wilson NC, Denton TX and Chesterfield VA. Their buildout is invisible in this ranking, so the top-10 understates where plants are being built; I did not recompute the ranking with them because their values are unpublished. Two entries ending in 999 (state-level unclassified aggregates) were also dropped from the chart.

Join caution (row fanout on geo.counties): a second query that attached county names via geo.counties duplicated each row about 16 times because the join omitted the table's year key. I used it only to read names and spot-check values; every figure quoted is a per-county value, not a sum, so duplication does not inflate them, and the ranking itself came from the un-joined first query.

QCEW counts staffed jobs, so it lags construction by years and shows completed or expanded plants, not sites under construction. I do not know what drives Union NJ's near doubling; none of my sources explain it.

Evidence of under construction (outside sources)

  • A UNC Kenan-Flagler table (dated Sept 2025) listed 17 sites broken ground or under construction, about $26.9B of disclosed investment: Novo Nordisk Clayton NC $4.1B, Lilly Lebanon IN $4.5B and Indianapolis $4.5B, Fujifilm Holly Springs $3.2B, J&J Wilson NC $2B, Biogen NC $2B, Amgen Holly Springs $1.5B, and others. That is roughly 5% to 7% of headline pledges, and stale by a year.
  • Fall 2026 groundbreakings: Lilly Houston, Genentech Hillsboro, Lilly Lehigh Valley (Lilly says 10 new US sites since 2020).
  • As of Nov 2025, analysts noted equipment makers saw little order flow and Repligen expected groundbreaking in 2026 to 2027 at the earliest.
  • DPR reports no meaningful generics reshoring interest, so the buildout is brand-name and biologics, not low-cost generic API.

Limits

I found no single public tracker separating announced from broken-ground from operating; the split above is stitched together from company releases, trade press and QCEW. The UNC table is a year old and not exhaustive. I did not obtain a pharma-specific Census construction-spending series.

What This Report Does Not Answer

  • API-specific split (vs finished dosage): NAICS 3254 does not separate API; API sites named from press only.
  • Current Nov 2026 status for every project: Only a subset verified for 2026; the under-construction list is from Sept 2025.

Every query behind this report

2 warehouse calls ran in this session, in order. Each is reproducible against the same snapshot.

query — 20 rows — 4591 ms
WITH a AS (SELECT area_fips, annual_avg_estabs e25, annual_avg_emplvl j25, disclosure_code d FROM econ.county_qcew WHERE year=2025 AND industry_code='3254' AND own_code='5'), b AS (SELECT area_fips, annual_avg_estabs e22, annual_avg_emplvl j22 FROM econ.county_qcew WHERE year=2022 AND industry_code='3254' AND own_code='5') SELECT a.area_fips, e22, e25, j22, j25, j25-j22 AS dj, d FROM a JOIN b USING(area_fips) WHERE j25 IS NOT NULL AND j22 IS NOT NULL ORDER BY j25-j22 DESC FETCH FIRST 20 ROWS ONLY
query — 464 rows — 4185 ms
SELECT c.area_fips, g.county_name, c."year", c.annual_avg_emplvl j, c.annual_avg_estabs e, c.disclosure_code d FROM econ.county_qcew c LEFT JOIN geo.counties g ON g.county_fips=c.area_fips WHERE c.industry_code='3254' AND c.own_code='5' AND c."year" IN (2022,2025) AND c.area_fips IN ('34039','18097','24031','17097','42091','39049','37063','37183','18011','37101','37195','37085','48201','48121','51041') ORDER BY c.area_fips, c."year"
