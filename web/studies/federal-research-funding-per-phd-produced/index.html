How much federal research funding goes into each doctorate the US produces, and how much does that vary by field?

Report: http://127.0.0.1:56817/a/c1edc90f87a9a04f4abe5c6cc469c237.html (local to the connector's machine; see dashboard.png in this folder for the board image)

Headline

FY2023: federal academic-research spending (NSF NCSES HERD survey) totaled $59.61B; US institutions awarded 57,806 research doctorates that year (NSF NCSES SED). National average: ~$1.03M in federal research dollars per doctorate produced — but this average hides an 18x spread across fields.

Life sciences gets ~18x more federal research dollars per doctorate produced than non-S&E fields.

How this was built

  • Numerator (federal $, by field): computed directly in the warehouse from research.nsf_herd_by_institution (HERD survey, funding_source='Federal', federal_agency='Total', FY2023), summed by rd_field. The nine field totals reconcile exactly to the reported "All fields" total ($59,610,006 thousand) — internal consistency check passed.
  • Denominator (doctorate counts, by field): not present in this warehouse (no SED table loaded); taken from NCES NCSES's own 2023 SED reporting and mapped onto HERD's field categories (closest reasonable alignment, not an official NSF crosswalk — see caveats).
  • Validation against literature: the federal total ($59.61B) matches independently reported HERD FY2023 figures ("almost $60 billion", NSF 25-313). The field ranking (life sciences and physical/earth sciences highest, non-S&E lowest) matches NSF's own linked SED/HERD funding-rate study (NSF 23-349: 36–51% of doctorate recipients get research funding, highest in physical/earth and life sciences, lowest in non-S&E) and the American Academy of Arts & Sciences' finding that FY2023 health-sciences R&D spending was 40x+ humanities R&D spending.

Caveats (also disclosed in the published report)

  • This is a resource-intensity ratio, not a literal cost-per-degree — HERD federal dollars fund faculty, postdocs, staff, and equipment, not just PhD students. NSF 23-349 finds only 36–51% of doctorate recipients personally receive research-grant funding.
  • HERD and SED use slightly different field taxonomies; the mapping here (e.g., health + agricultural sciences folded into "life sciences"; education/humanities/business folded into "non-S&E") is a best-effort alignment.
  • Psychology is HERD's own S&E category but non-S&E in some SED tabulations; kept separate here rather than folded in.
  • FY2023 used because it is the year both sides of the ratio are confirmed; HERD FY2024 ($64.6B federal) is loaded in the warehouse but wasn't paired with a confirmed 2024 SED field breakdown.
