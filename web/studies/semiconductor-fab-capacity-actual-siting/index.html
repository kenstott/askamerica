Semiconductor jobs have barely moved by county since 2022; new capacity shows first as construction, and the likely megafab counties are hidden by suppression or have thin labor pools

BLS QCEW (econ.county_qcew, private) 2022 vs 2025; ACS 5-year 2024. Proxy: NAICS 3344 employment, not announced-project data.

Summary

Reported semiconductor and electronic-component employment (NAICS 3344) is not yet landing as new fab jobs. Summed over counties with published figures, private 3344 employment went from 312,782 (2022) to 304,951 (2025), about -2.5%. The largest county gains are in established clusters: Santa Clara CA +2,166, Dallas TX +1,668, Anoka MN +910, Orange CA +881, Lake IL +721, Tarrant TX +675, Erie NY +661, Travis TX +539. The two biggest fab counties lost jobs: Maricopa AZ 23,967 to 22,036 and Washington OR 27,747 to 22,983. Onondaga NY (Micron's announced site) rose only 459 to 670. Licking OH (Intel's New Albany site) has one establishment and BLS suppresses its employment, so new megafab counties are largely invisible.

The 2022-25 signal is construction: Maricopa AZ added 5,403 nonresidential building construction jobs (16,141 to 21,544) and Travis TX 1,618. Harris TX and Nueces TX lead but reflect non-chip projects. Labor depth: Maricopa (labor force 2.38 million), Santa Clara (1.05 million), Travis, Williamson and Washington OR are deep; Licking OH (91,868 labor force, 6,875 in manufacturing, 2,968 unemployed) and Onondaga NY (239,494) are thin relative to a megafab and would rely on commuting and in-migration.

Exclusions and what they changed

The ranking SQL excluded counties where annual_avg_emplvl IS NOT NULL failed in either year (a.annual_avg_emplvl IS NOT NULL and b.annual_avg_emplvl IS NOT NULL): 588 of the 813 counties that carry a 3344 row in both years were excluded, leaving 225 ranked. BLS suppresses employment for single-employer cells (disclosure code N). The excluded counties are where one new plant would show, so the ranking understates new-fab counties. Excluded counties with the most establishments in 2025 include Summit OH (39095), New York NY (36061), Canyon ID (16005), Fairfax VA (51059), Jackson MO (29095), Sacramento CA (06067), Centre PA (42027), Denton TX (48121), Anne Arundel MD (24003) and Licking OH (39089). Headline with exclusions: -2.5% on published cells; without the exclusion the figure cannot be computed because the values are hidden, so treat it as a floor-level, composition-sensitive estimate.

Correction: I first examined Taylor County TX (48441, Abilene) as a Samsung site; Samsung's fab is in Williamson County (48491), whose 3344 jobs rose 793 to 1,216 (+423). Taylor County is not a fab county.

Caveats

  • No CHIPS Act or project data in the warehouse; project-county links are unverified background knowledge.
  • NAICS 3344 includes non-chip electronics; 2362 includes data centers and LNG.
  • ACS is a 2020-24 average with no engineer/technician detail by county, so depth reflects workforce size, not skills.
  • The 2022 to 2025 window straddles the August 2022 CHIPS Act; fabs finishing in 2026-28 will not appear yet.

What This Report Does Not Answer

  • Name announced projects explaining leaders: No CHIPS or project table in the warehouse; project-county links are unverified background knowledge.
