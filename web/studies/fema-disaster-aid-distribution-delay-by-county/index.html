DR-4933 (Indiana, declared 2026-08-25): six PA-declared counties show no federal money yet, and the slowest IA counties are Hancock, Putnam, Carroll, Decatur and Pike

OpenFEMA via AskAmerica disasters schema, loaded 2026-11-12; proxy for waiting time, not payment dates

Summary

The warehouse has no payment-date field, so true waiting time cannot be measured. As a proxy, I took the most recent large Individual Assistance (IA) disaster, Indiana DR-4933 (severe storms, tornadoes, flooding; declared 2026-08-25, about 80 days before today), and compared counties on what has reached residents so far. Two groups stand out. (1) Six Public Assistance (PA) counties have no project worksheets and no IA registrations in the data: Grant, Warren, Vermillion, Starke, Fountain and Parke. (2) Among counties with IA applicants, the lowest shares of registrants with any award are Hancock (24%), Putnam (26%), Carroll (33%), Decatur (34%) and Pike (35%), versus 79% in Fayette. Lake County (58%) has by far the largest absolute backlog: 44,462 registrations, about 18,567 with no award, and $28.8 million of FEMA-verified unmet need.

Small counties (under 60 registrations) have unstable percentages; Hancock, Putnam, Carroll, Decatur, Pike and Morgan rest on 9 to 58 applicants. The large counties with weak rates and real volume are Porter, LaPorte and Marion.

Public Assistance: Lake had $7.6M obligated of $10.1M approved (75%); most PA counties have only a few project worksheets worth $12K to $190K each. Grant, Warren, Vermillion, Starke, Fountain and Parke (all PA-declared) and 12 other PA counties (for example Marion, Pike, Carroll, Putnam, Union, Rush, Pulaski, Cass, Randolph, Fayette) have no PA worksheets yet.

Caveats

  • No disbursement timestamps exist in the loaded tables; 'waiting longest' is inferred from the share of applicants with no award and counties with zero records, as of the 2026-11-12 load. All counties share one declaration date, so the difference is in progress, not in elapsed days.
  • A zero-registration county may simply have had little damage or low take-up, not delay. A registrant with no award may have been found ineligible, insured, or still pending; the data cannot separate these.
  • PA money goes to governments, not directly to residents; IA is the residents-facing measure.
  • I chose DR-4933 as the most recent disaster with large IA volume; DR-4936 and DR-4941 (declared 2026-09-01) are newer but too small or have zero awards (DR-4941: 326 registrations, 0 awarded across 3 counties). If you meant a different disaster, the same method applies.
  • County-level, one disaster, no causal claim.

What This Report Does Not Answer

  • Rank affected counties by lag in aid reaching residents: No payment-date data; ranked on a proxy (share of registrants awarded, counties with no records).

query — 25 rows — 990 ms
SELECT d.disaster_number, d.declaration_title, MIN(d.declaration_date) dd, COUNT(DISTINCT d.county_fips) counties, MAX(CASE WHEN ih_program_declared THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) ih, MAX(CASE WHEN pa_program_declared THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) pa FROM disasters.disaster_declarations d WHERE d.declaration_date >= DATE '2025-07-01' AND declaration_type='DR' GROUP BY d.disaster_number, d.declaration_title ORDER BY dd DESC FETCH FIRST 25 ROWS ONLY
query — 20 rows — 2950 ms
SELECT r.disaster_number, MIN(d.declaration_date) dd, MIN(d.declaration_title) t, COUNT(DISTINCT r.county_fips) cty, SUM(r.registrations) reg, SUM(r.ihp_awarded_registrations) awarded, SUM(r.ihp_amount) amt FROM disasters.ihp_valid_registrations_by_county r JOIN disasters.disaster_declarations d ON d.disaster_number=r.disaster_number WHERE r.disaster_number>=4850 GROUP BY r.disaster_number ORDER BY r.disaster_number DESC FETCH FIRST 20 ROWS ONLY
query — 30 rows — 2228 ms
WITH dec AS (SELECT county_fips, MIN(designated_area) area, MAX(CASE WHEN pa_program_declared THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) pa FROM disasters.disaster_declarations WHERE disaster_number=4933 GROUP BY county_fips),
ia AS (SELECT county_fips, SUM(registrations) reg, SUM(ihp_awarded_registrations) aw, SUM(ihp_amount) amt, SUM(inspections_issued) insp, SUM(inspections_returned) insr, SUM(unmet_need_real_property+unmet_need_personal_property) unmet FROM disasters.ihp_valid_registrations_by_county WHERE disaster_number=4933 GROUP BY county_fips),
pa AS (SELECT county_fips, COUNT(*) pws, SUM(project_amount) proj, SUM(federal_share_obligated) obl FROM disasters.public_assistance_projects WHERE disaster_number=4933 GROUP BY county_fips)
SELECT dec.county_fips, dec.area, dec.pa, ia.reg, ia.aw, ROUND(100.0*ia.aw/NULLIF(ia.reg,0),1) pct_awarded, ROUND(ia.amt/NULLIF(ia.reg,0)) amt_per_reg, ROUND(100.0*ia.insr/NULLIF(ia.insp,0),1) insp_done, ia.unmet, pa.pws, pa.proj, pa.obl FROM dec LEFT JOIN ia USING(county_fips) LEFT JOIN pa USING(county_fips) ORDER BY pct_awarded NULLS FIRST
