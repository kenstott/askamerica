No dataset measures bank lending to tariff-exposed manufacturers; SBA loans give a proxy of about $11.3B over FY2021-25, led by California, Texas and Florida

Answer

The total cannot be measured from available data. Bank call reports and the Fed do not publish commercial loans by detailed manufacturing industry by state, and no import-input dependence series is loaded. As a partial proxy, SBA 7(a) and 504 approvals to manufacturers total $17.6B in FY2021-25 (about 9% of $196.5B of all SBA approvals). Of that, $11.3B (64%) went to 14 manufacturing subsectors I classed as import-input-heavy, and $6.7B to the core metals, machinery, electronics and transport-equipment group (NAICS 331-336).

By state (exposed set): California $1.85B (16%), Texas $0.86B (8%), Florida $0.78B (7%), Illinois $0.59B (5%), Ohio $0.54B (5%), then Minnesota $0.47B, Wisconsin $0.45B, New York $0.41B, Pennsylvania $0.40B, Michigan $0.38B. The top five hold about 41%; the top ten about 59%. These are mostly the largest manufacturing states, so the ranking largely mirrors manufacturing size.

Method and caveats

  • Source: fiscal.sba_loan_approvals, gross approved amounts, approval FY2021-2025, NAICS 31-33, by project_state.
  • Exposed set (my classification, not computed from the BEA import matrix): NAICS 313-316, 325, 326, 331-337, 339. Excluded as lower dependence: food, beverage, wood, paper, printing, petroleum, nonmetallic minerals.
  • SBA lending is small-business, guaranteed lending and a tiny slice of commercial bank lending; approvals, not outstanding balances. It will understate large-firm exposure. Shares are of this proxy only.
  • Not done: per-state exposure relative to state manufacturing output, and ranking sensitivity to the sector classification.
  • A real answer needs Fed SNC or bank-level C&I data by industry plus BEA import-matrix input shares.

What This Report Does Not Answer

  • Total commercial lending to import-input-dependent manufacturers: No public loan-by-industry-by-state series for all commercial bank lending exists in the corpus; SBA 7(a)/504 approvals used as a disclosed partial proxy.
  • Identify manufacturers heavily dependent on imported inputs: BEA import-matrix input shares are not loaded; the sector set is my judgment-based classification, not a computed measure.
