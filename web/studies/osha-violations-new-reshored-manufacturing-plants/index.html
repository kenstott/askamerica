No dataset here isolates new plants, but the public record shows real safety citations at new battery plants, with enforcement thinning in 2025

Sources: web reporting and a Senate release; AskAmerica catalog has no OSHA table. Retrieved 2026-11-20.

Answer

Partly, for batteries; it cannot be shown for tariff-driven reshoring plants as a group. The documented problems are at new EV-battery plants (Ultium Cells in Ohio, SK Battery in Georgia), mostly opened under 2022-era subsidies, before the 2025 tariffs. Federal OSHA also did about 20% fewer inspections in 2025, so few citations at newer plants would not prove few hazards. AskAmerica has no OSHA data (request filed), so I could not count violations across plants myself.

What the record shows

  • SK Battery America, Commerce GA (read directly): DOL proposed $77,200 in April 2024 after an October 2023 battery fire exposed 13 workers to respiratory hazards; second citation round in under six months; about 3,100 employees.
  • Ultium Cells (GM/LG), Warren OH (from search summaries, article not loaded): about $270,091 for 19 violations after a 2023 fire; company contested.
  • BlueOval SK, Kentucky (read directly): worker complaints about chemical hazard communication; three state-OSHA inspections Aug-Dec 2024 (per the same article); one outlet counts 47 battery-sector citations in 2024 vs 44 in 2023, unverified.

Enforcement backdrop

A Warren-led Senate Democrat release (read directly, Feb 2026) reports 20% fewer OSHA inspections and 42% fewer fines for severe violations in 2025, and cites a Good Jobs First analysis (not loaded) of 35% fewer cases and $94M in penalties, 47% lower. These are partisan sources. I found no manufacturing-specific totals.

Caveats

No comparison to established plants, no per-worker rates, no injury data; citations lag inspections by months. A real answer needs OSHA's inspection file joined to new-plant openings by NAICS, date and employment. A warehouse query for EPA-regulated battery/metal-product facilities (3,852 rows) exists, but it is environmental, not workplace-safety, data and was not used further.

What This Report Does Not Answer

  • Systematic count of violations at all tariff/reshoring-driven plants vs established plants: AskAmerica has no OSHA inspection/violation table (data request filed); no list of tariff-driven plants exists. Not answerable rigorously here.
  • Separate tariff-driven plants from earlier subsidy-driven plants: Documented cases are 2022-2024 battery plants, opened before the 2025 tariffs.
